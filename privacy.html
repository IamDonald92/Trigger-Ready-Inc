Privacy Policy

Trigger Ready · Last updated: July 2026

Trigger Ready ("we", "us") operates paintball experiences in Botswana, including kids' birthday sessions, corporate team building sessions, and in-home events. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you fill in one of our enquiry or booking forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, and your answers to questions about the event you are planning (for example the number of guests, ages, preferred venue, and dates). If you contact us on WhatsApp, by phone, or on social media, we also receive the information you share in those messages.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, arrange and manage bookings, communicate with you about your event, and — unless you ask us not to — occasionally tell you about our offers and events. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Forms on Facebook and Instagram

If you submit your details through a form on Facebook or Instagram, that information is also processed by Meta Platforms in line with Meta's own privacy policy, which you can read at facebook.com/privacy/policy. We download and store the details you submit so we can contact you.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as reasonably needed to serve you and to run our business, for example so we can assist you with future events. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except service providers we use to run the business (such as messaging and advertising platforms), or where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop receiving marketing messages from us. Just message us on WhatsApp or use the contact details below and we will act on your request.

Children

Our forms and marketing are directed at parents and adult organisers, not at children. Booking information about children (such as ages for a birthday party) is provided to us by their parent or guardian and used only to run the event safely.

Contact us
